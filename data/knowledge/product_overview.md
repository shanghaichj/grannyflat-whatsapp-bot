## Product focus

- Target market: Australian **granny flats / small second dwellings**.
- **Flagship / core:** **60 / 70 / 80 m²** modular granny flats (mostly **two-bedroom**; **A80 is three-bedroom**).
  - **Other states (NSW, VIC, SA, TAS, ACT, NT, etc.): ~60 m²** two-bed — https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/60
  - **WA: usually ~70 m²** two-bed — https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/70
  - **QLD: usually ~70 m²** — https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/70 (larger than 70 m² → 80 m² range: A80 3-bed / B80 2-bed+study / C80·D80 2-bed — https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/80)
- **Secondary:** Box studios **15 / 20 / 30 m²** (single module) if the customer wants compact — https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/15, https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/20, https://www.ausgrannyflat.com/m/30.
- Designs and build methods target **Class 1a** requirements, aiming for building approval and a final **Occupancy Certificate (OC)** with licensed local partners (lot/council-specific — do not guarantee permits in chat).
- Manufacturing approach: complete roughly **90%** of structure, MEP rough-in, and finishes in China as **modules**, then ship and assemble on site in Australia with limited local works.

## Modular delivery

- Module count, transport dimensions and lifting weights depend on the selected model and approved drawings. Confirm these before quoting logistics or crane capacity.
- Use only the selected official website plan; confirm module count, transport dimensions and weights from its approved drawings. No generic transport layout is a saleable floor plan.
- After arrival, modules move to site by **flatbed truck**; a **crane** places modules onto a prepared foundation.
- Modules connect with **bolted structural frames**; joint insulation and finishes completed on site.

## Factory completion level

Typically completed in the factory:

- Structural frame and main building envelope
- Most interior installation
- Bathroom fixtures, kitchen cabinets and sink (per delivery standard)
- Interior finishing largely complete

To support Australian inspection of services, some wall/floor sections may be left temporarily open for on-site plumbing/electrical completion and then sealed.

## On-site works (Australia)

Typical local scope:

1. Concrete foundation / slab with anchor bolts
2. Underground electrical conduits and water pipes outside the building
3. Crane lift and module positioning
4. Licensed plumber and electrician connections; duration depends on scope and site, with no fixed duration quoted in chat
5. Joint finishing between modules
6. Permits / Occupancy Permit pathway via local partners

## Materials (summary)

- Light steel frame, OSB, insulation, fibre-cement exterior, gypsum interiors
- Bathroom: ceramic/SPC floors, cement-based WPC wall panels, standard sanitary set
- Flat / low-slope roof for drainage; WPC or similar flooring with acoustic layers
- Electrical and plumbing materials specified to meet Australian standards (final on-site connection by licensed trades)
