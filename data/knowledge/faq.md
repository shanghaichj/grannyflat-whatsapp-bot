## Class 1a / Occupancy Certificate (OC)

Our designs and construction methods are prepared to **Class 1a** requirements, aiming for a pathway that can obtain building approval and a final **OC** with licensed local builders/certifiers. Always confirm against the specific lot and council — do not promise a guaranteed approval in chat.

## How is it shipped?

Modules ship by **sea** (container or break-bulk depending on configuration) to ports such as **Sydney, Brisbane, Melbourne, Perth**, then trucked to site (planning assumption often **within ~200 km** of port — longer hauls need a custom freight quote).

## What happens on site?

Foundation/slab, crane placement, module joining, licensed plumbing and electrical connections, and joint finishing. Local builder manages construction procedures and helps with Occupancy Permit.

## Do I need council / planning approval?

**Almost always you need a building permit pathway.** Planning rules differ by state and overlays.

### Victoria note (Small Second Dwelling)

Victoria’s **Small Second Dwelling** rules (post VC253) are often discussed as:

- Gross floor area typically capped around **60 m²** for the SSD pathway
- Must provide kitchen sink, food prep, bath/shower, toilet, basin
- **All-electric** — no reticulated natural gas connection under that pathway
- **No subdivision** of the secondary dwelling onto its own title
- On many standard residential lots **≥300 m²**, planning permit may not be required unless overlays (heritage, flood, etc.) apply; **lots under 300 m²** often still need a planning permit
- **Building permit** is still required (Class 1a)

Always verify against current council / VBA guidance for the specific lot — confirm with the customer’s suburb rather than certifying eligibility in chat.

Useful official starting points:

- VIC planning — Small Second Dwellings: https://www.planning.vic.gov.au/guides-and-resources/strategies-and-initiatives/small-second-dwellings
- VBA small second homes: https://www.vba.vic.gov.au/consumers/small-second-homes
- NCC: https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/

## Import / compliance red lines

- **Asbestos-free** evidence (NATA lab) — critical for Australian Border Force
- **Engineered stone ban** — do not specify engineered stone benchtops
- Timber packaging/components may need **fumigation** / biosecurity compliance
- Structure must be certifiable to Australian standards (e.g. AS 1170 wind/seismic/soil) by a **local registered structural engineer**
- Windows/glazing and energy performance must meet NCC / NatHERS expectations (often discussed as **7-star** under NCC 2022 — confirm project specifics)
- On-site licensed plumber/electrician certification is mandatory for services

## Can it be rented?

Often yes as a secondary dwelling, but short-stay and zoning overlays vary. Victoria SSD notes commonly allow rental / family use but **not** title subdivision. Confirm locally.
